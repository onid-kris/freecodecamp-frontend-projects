Our Travel Destinations

The Philippines boasts three notable capital cities, each offering its own charm and history. Manila, the national capital, blends Spanish colonial heritage with modern city life, featuring landmarks like Intramuros and Rizal Park. Quezon City, the former capital, is a vibrant hub for culture, education, and entertainment, home to the Quezon Memorial Circle and bustling food districts. Cebu City, the “Queen City of the South” and the nation first capital, offers a rich mix of historical sites like Magellan Cross and stunning nearby beaches, making it a perfect gateway to the Visayas. Together, these cities showcase the Philippines diverse culture, history, and beauty.
