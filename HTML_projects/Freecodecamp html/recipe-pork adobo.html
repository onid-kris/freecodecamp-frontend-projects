Pork Adobo Recipe

Pork Adobo is a iconic Filipino comfort food, characterized as a savory and slightly tangy stew where pork—typically belly or shoulder—is slow-cooked in a mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. It is often regarded as the unofficial national dish of the Philippines, loved for its tender, melt-in-the-mouth texture and rich, reduced glaze-like sauce

Ingredients

  • Pork Belly
  • Soy sauce
  • Dry bay leaves
  • White Vinegar
  • Whole peppercorns
  • garlic
  • 1 Cup of Water

Instructions

  1. Combine pork, soy sauce, and garlic in a bowl and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sear the pork in oil over medium-high heat until browned, reserving the marinade.
  3. Add the reserved marinade, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 45 minutes to an hour, or until pork is tender.
  4. Pour in the vinegar and let it boil uncovered for 3-5 minutes without stirring.
  5. Simmer uncovered until the sauce is reduced and oil separates. Serve with rice.
